Inside Bootcamp Track

More than a logbook.

Track is built for serious training. Every strength session logged makes your coaching smarter and your programming more precise.

👤
Your Member Profile

Every member gets a personal profile page showing their Big Three headline lifts, progress over time, and full session history. See exactly where you started and how far you've come.

🧮
1RM & Percentage Calculator

The app automatically calculates your estimated one-rep max from every session. Your profile then shows working weights at every percentage from 60% to 100% in 5% steps — so you always know exactly what to load.

🏆
Automatic PB Detection

Every time you log a lift, Track compares it to your previous best. Beat it and the app flags it — immediately in the app and in the master coaching spreadsheet in real time. PBs never get forgotten again.

📊
Live Coaching Dashboard

Coaches get a full dashboard with a member leaderboard, activity tracker, and aggregate stats across the gym. Everything updates automatically as members log their sessions.

📐
Percentage Calculator — Always Ready

Based on your most recent 1RM, Track instantly shows you the exact weight for every training percentage. No maths, no guessing. Walk into every session knowing exactly what's on the bar.

Questions

Common questions.

Do I need to be an existing member?
No — you don't need to be a Bewdley Bootcamp member to sign up for these apps. Anyone can benefit from them.
How do I log in after subscribing?
Just enter your email on the login screen and we'll send you a magic link — no password needed. Click the link and you're straight in. Same email works for both apps.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Cancel any time and access continues until the end of that billing period. No questions, no hassle.
How does the 1RM calculator work?
Every time you log a lift, Track uses your weight and reps to estimate your one-rep max automatically. Your profile then shows working weights from 60% to 100% in 5% increments based on that number.
When are new recipes added?
Five new main meal recipes appear in the app every month automatically. No updates needed — just open the app and they're there.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Workout data and preferences are stored securely via Supabase. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe — we never see your card details.
